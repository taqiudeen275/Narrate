## Device recommendations

Settings → Voice models now recommends a supported Kokoro edition using coarse
memory/CPU hints and local generation measurements. The default is q8 because
its actual weights are the smallest (about 88 MiB); q4 is about 291 MiB and fp32
about 310 MiB. A lower bit label alone does not promise a smaller or faster file.
Measured speed can change the recommendation when estimated memory permits it.

Only generation at 1× pace is compared. After at least three sentences, the panel
estimates generation time, excluding model loading. Slower-than-playback devices
are advised to render first. Missing hardware hints, temperature, and other apps
are handled as uncertainty. Timing totals remain in local IndexedDB; document
text and device inventory are not uploaded for recommendations.
